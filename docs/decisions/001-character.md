# 001 · The hero: Chuski, a drop of chai

**Status:** decided (veto any time) · **Date:** 2 Oct 2026 · Guide §1, §2, §7

## Decision

**Chuski** (CHOOS-kee, from *chai ki chuski*, "a sip of chai") is a round, resting drop of milky
masala chai. A wisp of white steam floats above its head. It's a **Level 1 blob**: no limbs. It
moves by hopping, squashing, wobbling and literally melting into a puddle.

- **Personality:** warm · clingy · dramatic
- **Flaw:** panics about going cold. Ignored for a minute, it shivers, its steam fades and a
  wrinkly *malai* skin forms on its head like a sad little hat.
- **Signature move:** the *steam swirl*, a happy spin that curls its steam into a heart
- **Signature sound:** "plip!" (gibberish only, so it works in every language)
- **Biggest fear:** the microwave. Reheated chai is a crime.

## Why this one

1. **Under-used, with huge built-in relatability.** Chai runs through Indian daily life: morning
   cups, 4 PM office breaks, exam nights, guests, rain. 3D *characters* built on it are rare,
   while cats, capybaras, axolotls, frogs and platypuses are crowded (guide §7.1).
2. **A liquid justifies every cartoon rule.** Squash and stretch, wobble, splitting in two,
   melting into a puddle and boiling over are all physical facts here. The comedy comes from the
   material itself (guide §11.2).
3. **The steam is a free emotion meter.** It works as Chuski's eyebrow: a heart when happy, a droop
   when sad, puffs when angry, gone when cold. That stays readable at thumbnail size.
4. **Fastest to build with scripts:** one subdivided sphere, two eyes, blush, a mouth and a curve
   (guide §7.3 Level 1, §8.2).
5. **Baby schema built in** (§2): the whole body is a head, eyes sit low (42% of the height) and
   wide, it's all circles, the features are tiny, and the palette is pastel with a soft
   subsurface skin.
6. **Works as a portfolio piece.** A food mascot with a strong personality is exactly what D2C
   brands, cafés and tech companies hire for (§19.1).

## Alternatives considered

| Idea | Why not |
|---|---|
| Pani puri | Cute and fragile, but "about to be eaten" gags get morbid fast |
| Laddoo | Perfect sphere, but mostly festival-only gags |
| Monsoon cloud | Lovely, but cloud characters are common in cute illustration |
| Kulhad (clay cup) with a face | Cup characters are crowded, and the cup shape is less blobby |
| Pressure cooker ("3 whistles") | Very funny, but metallic and shiny goes against baby schema. Kept as a future guest prop |
| Samosa | A triangle reads as "danger" (§2 shape language) |
| Bug Gremlin (command-center idea) | No local flavour. Better as a client-demo mascot later |

## Risks and how they're handled

- **Silhouette risk:** a brown teardrop with a curl on top could read as the poop emoji. So the
  body is a **wide dome, not a teardrop**, the colour is a **light milky peach, not brown**, and
  the steam is **white, translucent and floating separately**. Phase 2 must pass this check at thumbnail size.
- **Water-drop mascots exist generically** (utilities, brands). The chai colour, the steam, the
  malai gag and the sugar-cube partner make this one specific.
- **Not verified:** real hashtag volumes (Instagram blocks automated checks). "Chai" memes are
  huge, but I couldn't measure how many accounts use chai *characters*. Spend 10 minutes
  searching #chai and #chailover Reels to sanity-check.

## How to veto

Comment `veto: <why>` on the Phase 1 PR. A different character changes `brand/*.json`, the
sheets and the episode list, but no code.
