# 002 · The sidekick: Shakkar, a grumpy sugar cube

**Status:** decided (veto any time) · **Date:** 2 Oct 2026 · Guide §2, §7.1

## Decision

**Shakkar** (Hindi/Urdu for sugar) is a small, slightly rounded **sugar cube**: crystal-white,
grumpy and proper. He's about half Chuski's height. He moves by **tumbling edge over edge**, a
cube's walk.

- **Personality:** grumpy · proper · secretly sweet
- **Flaw:** terrified of dissolving, so he avoids anything warm. He's also Chuski's best friend.
- **Signature move:** the *corner melt*. Warmth or kindness softens his sharp corners for a moment,
  and he hastily squares himself up again.
- **Signature sound:** a crystal "krrsh" and a disapproving "hmph"

## Why

- **Opposites make endless gags** (§7.1): Chuski wants hugs, and a hug dissolves Shakkar. Every
  episode can turn on the push-pull of a best friend he physically can't get close to.
- **Shape contrast** (§2): Chuski is all circles; Shakkar is a square, the shape of stubbornness.
  It's funny at a glance, even in silhouette.
- **Colour contrast:** crystal white against chai peach, unlike a toast or rusk sidekick, which
  would be brown on brown.
- **Very Indian, also universal.** "Cheeni kitni?" ("how much sugar?") is asked in every Indian
  home, and sugar cubes are understood everywhere.
- **The simplest model possible:** a bevelled cube. Tumbling is a pure rotation about an edge,
  easy to script and very readable.

## Notes

- I avoided the name "Cheeni" (also sugar) because it's also used colloquially for "Chinese",
  which risks being read as an ethnic jab.
- Shakkar's resting face is half-lidded and grumpy, but he still has round, low-ish eyes and a
  blush that shows when flustered, so he stays cute (eyes at 52% of his height).
