# 005 · Content strategy and schedule

**Status:** decided · **Date:** 2 Oct 2026 · Guide §5, §16, §17.3, §18

## Schedule you can sustain

- **One episode every Saturday.** Launch on the first Saturday after **three finished episodes**
  are in reserve (§17.3). Target launch: **Sat 14 Nov 2026**, if Phases 2–7 are merged by about 31 Oct.
- **Optional Wednesday bonus:** a 5–8 s perfect loop or a behind-the-scenes clip. Only when
  ahead of schedule, never at the cost of Saturday.
- **Your time per episode** once the pipeline exists: about 10 min to approve the storyboard,
  0–60 min to hand-polish one key shot (optional, H08), 10 min for the phone check and posting,
  and 15–30 min for comments. That's roughly **1–2 h/week**, inside the light plan (§5).

## Weekly rotation (4-week cycle)

| Week | Slot | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Relatable life** (§16.2 pillar 2) | The most sendable: sends per reach is the strongest signal (§1) |
| 2 | **Duo gag**: Chuski vs Shakkar (pillar 6) | Builds the relationship people follow |
| 3 | **Interactive** or **Food & ASMR**, alternating (pillars 3, 4) | Comments, replays, saves |
| 4 | **Trend**, **Perfect loop** or **Behind the scenes**, rotating (pillars 1, 7, 8) | Reach (trends), replays (loops), client proof (BTS) |

**Seasonal override (pillar 5):** a festival or season within 7 days takes that week's slot.
Production starts **two weeks before** the post date (§16.2), and `growth/calendar.csv` lists both
dates.

## The pillars, adapted to Chuski

1. **Trend:** Chuski and Shakkar do the current sound or meme (Phase 8 fast-path, minutes not hours).
2. **Relatable:** Indian daily life through chai: alarms, 4 PM breaks, guests, reheating, exams.
3. **Interactive:** "pause to pick the sugar", "which glass is Chuski in?", "count the bubbles".
4. **Food & ASMR:** boil-overs, the long pour, stirring, bubbling, crunch.
5. **Seasonal:** monsoon (core), winter "chai season", Diwali, Holi, New Year, International Tea Day (21 May).
6. **Duo:** hugs that dissolve, sugar arguments, personal space, sharing a glass.
7. **Perfect loops:** steam swirl, rain watching, sleepy breathing, boiling idle.
8. **Behind the scenes:** clay → final, "the code behind the hop", the character noticing the render bar.

## Rules that keep it "authentic" for YouTube and Instagram (§20)

- Every episode has a **new gag**, new poses and its own sound plan. Never the same gag reskinned.
  The Phase 7 QA guard compares beat structures to recent episodes.
- No fixed visual template: each episode changes the camera, the props or the time of day.
- Themes are teen/adult (work, college, home), so episodes are **not made for kids** (§21).
