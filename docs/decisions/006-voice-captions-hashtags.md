# 006 · Brand voice, captions, hooks, hashtags, and "never do"

**Status:** decided · **Date:** 2 Oct 2026 · Guide §15.1, §16.4, §18.2, §21

## Voice

Warm, short, a little dramatic, never mean. Lowercase hooks. Light Hinglish where every
viewer still gets it ("bas 5 minute", "cheeni kitni?"). One or two emojis at most. The characters
never speak real words on screen; captions do the talking.

## Hook-text formulas (frame 1, inside the safe zone, about 1 s per 3 words)

- `me when ___` · `POV: ___` · `4 PM be like`
- `send this to your ___` (only for genuinely relatable episodes)
- `wait for it…` (**only** when the payoff is real; otherwise it erodes trust)
- `pause to ___` (interactive episodes)
- `day N of ___` (series)
- `chai ki ___` (brand series, e.g. "chai ki problems")

## Caption template

```
{one line in Chuski's voice} {question CTA}

#chuski #3danimation #blender3d #cuteanimation #{topic}
```

Example: *the microwave is NOT an option 😤 how do you rescue cold chai?*

Alt text: describe the gag plainly, e.g. "A small drop of chai with a steam curl gets cold and a
milk skin forms on its head."

## Hashtags (maximum 5, verified Instagram cap)

| Set | Tags |
|---|---|
| Core (always 4) | `#chuski` `#3danimation` `#blender3d` `#cuteanimation` |
| Topic (pick 1) | `#chai` · `#chailover` · `#relatable` · `#monsoon` · `#diwali` · `#holi` · `#asmr` · `#interactive` |

YouTube: the same 5 in the description (`#shorts` is optional, §18.2).

## Never do

- Real brands or logos (no named biscuits, tea brands or cafés) unless it's a paid, disclosed
  partnership.
- Copyrighted characters, or music baked into exports (Instagram trending audio goes in in-app only, §14.4).
- Religious figures, rituals as punchlines, politics, or national symbols.
- Jokes about people's caste, class or jobs (e.g. "chaiwala" stereotypes), and body- or diet-shaming.
  Shakkar's "less sugar" fear is about *him*, never about viewers' bodies.
- Toddler or nursery themes. Keep it not made for kids.
- Realistic AI footage, cloned voices, reposting others' clips, or "like if…" engagement bait.
- More than 5 hashtags, or a sponsored post without an upfront ASCI disclosure.
